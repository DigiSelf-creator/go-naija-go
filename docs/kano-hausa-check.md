# Kano: Hausa lines and street names for Tp's speaker check (decision 91)

qa K7, 25 Nov. Taken from the code as it ships (src/worlds/kano/words.ts and the shop signs in
src/worlds/kano/kurmi.ts, kofarMata.ts and dawanau.ts). Before the closed invited test, Tp or a
Hausa speaker Tp trusts reads each line and ticks it, or writes a better one.

For each line: is it right, is it kind, does it sound natural for Kano, and does it name God?
No line may name God (KN-R7).

## The three street names

- [ ] **Kurmi** (street 1): the old Kurmi market by the gate, famous for leather.
- [ ] **Kofar Mata** (street 2): the dye pits. The name is an old gate's name ("Women's Gate").
- [ ] **Dawanau** (street 3): the big grain market.

## Hausa words people say (speech bubbles)

- [ ] **"Barka da zuwa!"**: "Welcome!" (by the gate on Kurmi)
- [ ] **"Ina kwana?"**: "Good morning" (Kurmi, the morning street)
- [ ] **"Barka da rana!"**: "Good afternoon!" (Kofar Mata, the midday street)
- [ ] **"Barka da yamma!"**: "Good evening!" (Dawanau, the evening street)
- [ ] **"Sannu!"**: "Hello!" (any street)
- [ ] **"Lafiya lau!"**: "I'm fine, thanks!" (an answer)
- [ ] **"Na gode!"**: "Thank you!" (an answer)
- [ ] **"Ga fura!"**: "Here's fura!" (a fura seller on Kofar Mata)
- [ ] **"Ga gyada!"**: "Here are groundnuts!" (a groundnut trader on Dawanau)
- [ ] **"Yauwa!"**: "Great!" (a cheer; also the Borehole Hall door people on each glug)
- [ ] **"Madalla!"**: "Well done!" (a cheer, already used in Lagos and Ogun). Please check it
      does not read as "thanks be to God" to a Kano listener (KN-R7); if it does, we swap it.
- [ ] **"Sannu da aiki!"**: "Well done at your work!" (said to dyers and porters)
- [ ] **"Sai an jima!"**: "See you later!" (at the bus)

## Hausa words on shop signs and stalls (painted in capitals)

- [ ] **"GA FURA"**: "Here's fura" (Kofar Mata stall)
- [ ] **"GA GYADA"**: "Here are groundnuts" (Dawanau stall)
- [ ] **"KULI-KULI"**: the crunchy groundnut snack (Dawanau sign)
- [ ] **"FURA DA NONO"**: millet balls with fresh milk (Kofar Mata sign)

## Food names (shown in toasts, the HUD and on signs)

- [ ] **Kilishi**: thin, spiced dried meat (Kano power-up: "Kilishi! Wind can't hold you back.")
- [ ] **Fura da nono**: millet balls with fresh milk (power-up; the HUD says "Fura")
- [ ] **Kosai**: fried bean cakes ("Hot kosai!", a Kurmi seller)
