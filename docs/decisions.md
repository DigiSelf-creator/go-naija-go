# Decisions

Owned by the product owner, Tp. Newest first. Only Tp adds to this file.

6 Nov 2026 (Tp, in chat): the player controls movement, and controls when to throw fufu and use the staff. No auto-run.

6 Nov 2026 (Tp, in chat): the game is called Go Naija Go.

Starting decisions drafted 6 Nov 2026 from Tp's idea. Tp: delete or change any you disagree with before the first session.

1. The game plays like a side-scrolling platformer but is fully original: own name, heroes, art, sound and levels.
2. Bosses are problems (traffic, potholes, power cuts, red tape), never real people.
3. Power-ups are Nigerian food. Version 1: roasted corn, garri with sugar and epa, amala with gbegiri and ewedu.
4. Version 1 is a free web game, no accounts, no ads, no photo, saves on the phone.
