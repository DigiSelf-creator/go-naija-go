# Open questions for the product owner

Format: question, options, recommended option, what is blocked.

1. Is this the story?
   - The Unity Drum is broken into pieces by Mister Red Tape and his gang. The hero wins one piece per world, and the drum beats again. See docs/brief.md, The story.
   - Options: keep it; change the prize (for example a stolen national treasure, or a lost family member); change the villain.
   - Recommended: keep it. It explains every world, ties the bosses together, and ends on "Go, Naija, go" without naming any real person.
   - Blocks: story panels and the win screen words.

2. How should the herder's staff be named and shown?
   - Herding staffs are proud tools of Hausa and Fulani herders. Herding is also a sensitive topic in some parts of the country.
   - Options: "Herder's staff (sanda)"; "Sanda"; a plain "Walking staff".
   - Recommended: "Herder's staff (sanda)", shown with pride, used only to swat things away, never to hit animals. Ask two or three Hausa and Fulani speakers to check the name and look before release.
   - Blocks: the power-up name only.

3. Which foods come next, so every region feels at home?
   - Options to choose from: suya, puff-puff, zobo, kunu, tuwo shinkafa with miyan kuka, fura da nono, abacha, ofe nsala, banga soup with starch, boli and groundnut, akara, moi moi.
   - Recommended: one food from each region per new world, chosen with people from that region.
   - Blocks: nothing in version 1.

4. Should players be able to use a selfie later?
   - Options: never; later, turned into a cartoon on the phone and the photo thrown away, adults only; later, any age.
   - Recommended: later, on the phone only, adults only, after a privacy check under Nigeria's data protection law.
   - Blocks: nothing in version 1.

5. How will the game make money after the test?
   - Options: rewarded ads; small outfit packs in naira; a sponsor per world; a school edition; grants.
   - Recommended: start with a sponsor per world plus rewarded ads. Add outfit packs once players come back daily.
   - Blocks: nothing in version 1.
