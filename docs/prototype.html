◀ ▶ to move, ⤒ to jump (press again in the air for a second jump, hold to go higher), ✋ to use what you are holding. On a keyboard: arrow keys, space to jump, X to use.

  • 🌽 Roasted corn: run faster
  • 🥣 Garri, sugar and epa: a shield that takes one hit
  • 🍲 Amala, gbegiri and ewedu: super strong, smash through anything
  • ⚪ Pepper fufu: 8 hot fufu balls. Press ✋ to throw one where you face. Throw in the air to aim down.
  • 🦯 Herder's staff (sanda): press ✋ on the ground to swing and clear the path, press ✋ in the air to vault far over wide potholes. If you get hit, you drop the staff instead of losing a heart.
