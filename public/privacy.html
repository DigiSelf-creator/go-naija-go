Gbese! privacy

Short version: Gbese! does not collect anything about you. Your game stays on your phone.

What we do not do

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads.
  • No trackers and no analytics.
  • No photos, camera, microphone, contacts or location.
  • We do not collect, sell or share any data about you.

Your saved game

Your hero, your progress and your settings (like Sound and Gentle mode) are saved on this phone only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

In the app, phone backup is turned off for the game, so your save is not copied to a cloud backup. If you delete the app, your save is deleted too.

Tilt to steer

If you switch on tilt, the game reads how the phone leans, only while you play, only to steer. Nothing about it is saved or sent.

The internet

The app plays with no internet at all. The web test version downloads the game's own files from this site, then plays offline. Like any website, the company that hosts this site may keep normal visit records; we do not use them.

Children

Gbese! is made for everyone aged 10 and up. Because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Questions

Write to the contact email on the game's store page.
